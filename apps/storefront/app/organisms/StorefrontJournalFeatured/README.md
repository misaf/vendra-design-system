# StorefrontJournalFeatured

Composes the featured journal BlogCard from explicit title/image/excerpt/meta/cta/href props. Preserves the featured wrapper, wide layout, arch frame and priority image without adding a wrapper.

View retains visibility and localized/story descriptor mapping. Journal logic retains filtering, lead selection, image resolution, localized metadata, pagination and article hrefs. StorefrontJournalLayout retains slot placement; BlogCard retains presentation and link/image semantics. This organism owns no state, calculations, persistence or browser effects.

Verify bilingual lead content, priority image, category/search lead changes, later-page/loading/empty visibility, touch/keyboard article navigation, responsive containment, accessibility and screenshots against the previous version.
