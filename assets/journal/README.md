# Demo journal photography

Twelve editorial images generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation on 18 October 2026: ten distinct article covers and two supporting studio/bridal photographs. They illustrate fictional sample stories, not a real shop or its stock. Responsive WebP exports are 1200×900 and 480×360; original generated files remain outside the repository. The bridal guide also reuses the existing wedding-car category image for its car-flower caption.

The exact shared prompt and individual subject prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json). The image helper resolves both full-size and responsive URLs against the deployed storefront's asset base. Cards use decorative images beside linked titles; article body images retain their bilingual captions as alternative text.
