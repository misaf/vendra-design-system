Swipeable product photos with scroll-snap and pill dots (active widens). RTL-aware.
```jsx
<Gallery label="Photos" images={[{src:a,alt:'Front'},{src:b,alt:'Detail'}]} />
```

## Usage
**Use when:** Product detail photos — cover plus thumbnails.

**Don’t use when:** Don’t use for single images (ArchFrame) or horizontal product rows (SnapScroller).
